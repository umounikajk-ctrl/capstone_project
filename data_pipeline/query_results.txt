Query 5 - GROUP BY/HAVING
============================================================
SQL:
SELECT
    category_id,
    COUNT(*) AS category_count
FROM books
GROUP BY category_id
HAVING COUNT(*) > 10
ORDER BY category_count DESC;

Output:
 category_id  category_count
           4              48
           2              32
           6              26
           3              19
           5              13
           1              11

Query 6 - JOIN
============================================================
SQL:
SELECT
    b.title,
    c.category_name,
    b.rating,
    b.price_gbp,
    b.price_inr
FROM books AS b
JOIN categories AS c
ON b.category_id = c.category_id
ORDER BY b.rating DESC, b.title ASC
LIMIT 10;
